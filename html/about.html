STUDENT HEALTHCARE

Student Health Record & Clinic Management System

The Student Health Record & Clinic Management System is designed to help the campus clinic efficiently manage student health records, consultations, and other clinic-related information.

The system provides an organized and convenient way to store and manage healthcare information. It helps clinic personnel access important records while keeping student information properly organized.

✓

Our Goal

To provide a convenient and organized way to handle student healthcare services within the institution.

WHY SHRCMS?

Making Student Healthcare Easier

SHRCMS helps improve the way health information and clinic services are managed within the school.

Organized Records

Student health information can be stored and organized in one system for easier access.

Better Clinic Service

Clinic personnel can manage consultations and student healthcare information more efficiently.

Secure Information

Authorized personnel can access healthcare information according to their assigned role.
